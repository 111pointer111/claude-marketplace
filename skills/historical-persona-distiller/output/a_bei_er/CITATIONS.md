## 二、对分析严格性的批评

### C3. 献身纯数学（1826）
> "My work in the future must be devoted entirely to pure mathematics in its abstract meaning."
**出处：** Letter to Christoffer Hansteen (1826)

### C4. 当时分析缺乏计划与系统
> 分析"lacks so completely all plan and system"，人们采用"this miserable way of concluding from the special to the general."
**出处：** Letter to Christoffer Hansteen (1826), quoted by Øystein Ore and Morris Kline

## 三、对发散级数的批判

### C5. 发散级数是魔鬼的工作（1826）
> "On the whole, Divergent series are the work of the Devil and it's a Shame that one dares base any Demonstration on them. You can get whatever result you want when you use them."
**出处：** Letter to Bernt Michael Holmboe (January 1826), quoted by Arild Stubhaug
**回溯：** raw/a_bei_er/引语.txt 第 5 条

### C6. 发散级数的反例

**出处：** Same source
