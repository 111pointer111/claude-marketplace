## 一、需求层次理论

### C1. 自我实现作为根本命令(1954)
> "What a man can be, he must be. This need we may call self-actualization."
**出处:** Motivation and Personality (1954), Ch. 5
**回溯:** raw/ma_si_luo/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 高等需要的必要性(1954)
> "It is as necessary to the human being to have needs for the higher things as it is to have needs for the lower things."
**出处:** Motivation and Personality (1954), Ch. 5
**回溯:** raw/ma_si_luo/引语.txt 第 2 条

### C3. 人类生物学是积极的(1954)
> "Human biology is not just negative, not just the absence of disease. It is also the presence of something positive — the striving toward growth."
**出处:** Motivation and Personality (1954)
**回溯:** raw/ma_si_luo/引语.txt 第 3 条

## 二、高峰体验

### C4. 高峰体验作为限度的体验(1962)
> "The peak experience is a kind of oceanic feeling, an experience of limitlessness, of being at the boundary of the possible."
**出处:** Toward a Psychology of Being (1962)
**回溯:** raw/ma_si_luo/引语.txt 第 4 条

### C5. 高峰体验的转化效果(1971, posthumous)
> "We may not always be aware of the peak experiences in our lives, but they have a transforming effect on us."
**出处:** The Farther Reaches of Human Nature (1971, posthumous)
**回溯:** raw/ma_si_luo/引语.txt 第 5 条

### C6. 自我实现者无内疚(1968)

### C7. 人类低估自己(ca. 1962)

**回溯:** raw/ma_si_luo/引语.txt 第 8 条

### C9. 自我实现不是终点(ca. 1967)

> "Maslow was the first to take seriously the idea that the goal of psychology is not just to understand pathology, but to understand health, growth, and the highest reaches of human potential."

**回溯:** raw/ma_si_luo/后世评述.txt 第 1 条
