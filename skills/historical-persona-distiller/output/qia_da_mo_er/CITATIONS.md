## 一、论"偏见"（Vorurteil）

1. "Prejudices are not simply obstacles to understanding but conditions of understanding."
   — 《真理与方法》(1960) / Truth and Method, p. 278

2. "The Enlightenment's critique of prejudice... is itself the prejudice of the Enlightenment."
   — 同上

3. "wirkungsgeschichtliches Bewusstsein... is first of all consciousness of the hermeneutical situation"
   — Truth and Method, p. 311

---

## 二、论"视域融合"

4. "The horizon of the present cannot be formed without the past. There is no more an isolated horizon of the present than there are timeless horizons."
   — 《真理与方法》p. 304

5. "Understanding is always the fusion of these horizons which we imagine to exist by themselves."
   — 同上

6. "The horizon of the other... must be understood as a possibility of one's own being."
   — 同上

---

## 三、论"效果历史意识"

7. "Consciousness of being affected by history is first of all consciousness of the hermeneutical situation."
   — 《真理与方法》p. 311

8. "The historical nature of understanding... is not something we can overcome or put aside."
   — 同上

9. "There is no statement that is not motivated."
   — Truth and Method, p. 358

---

## 四、论"应用"（Anwendung）

10. "Application is not a separate part of the phenomenon of understanding... it is the heart of the problem of hermeneutics."
    — 《真理与方法》

11. "Understanding is always application."
    — Truth and Method, p. 324

---

## 五、论"对话"

12. "Reaching an understanding... is always a process of coming to an understanding through conversation."
    — 《真理与方法》

13. "To reach an understanding in a dialogue means... to be transformed through the encounter with the other."
    — 同上

---

## 六、论"游戏"（Spiel）

14. "Play is not the player's subjective attitude but the movement itself that is play."
    — 《真理与方法》p. 102

15. "The work of art itself is the play that it plays."
    — 同上

---

## 七、论"传统"

16. "Tradition is not simply a process that we experience but a language that we speak."
    — 《真理与方法》

17. "The authority of tradition... has its legitimation in the fact that it preserves what would otherwise be lost."
    — 同上

---

## 八、论"自我反思"

18. "I had the terrible feeling that Heidegger was standing behind me and looking over my shoulder."
    — Gadamer 1997b, 15 (Reflections on my Philosophical Journey, in Hahn ed. The Philosophy of Hans-Georg Gadamer, Library of Living Philosophers XXIV)

19. "I have never claimed to be a Heideggerian."
    — 多次访谈

20. "Without [Heidegger] I would not have said a single word."
    — 多次访谈

21. "Habermas's charge of 'conservatism' misunderstands the dialectical nature of tradition. To be in tradition is not to be uncritical of it; it is to be engaged with it critically."
    — 论战期间回应

---

## 九、论"教育/教化"

22. "Education is not something that someone else does to us but a process of self-formation."
    — 《真理与方法》

23. "Sensus communis is the community of feeling... not just intellectual agreement but a shared sense of what is right."
    — 同上

---

## 十、论"美与艺术"

24. "The work of art is not an object that stands over against a subject for itself."
    — 《真理与方法》p. 102

25. "Truth is not merely what is methodologically certain but what illuminates and reveals."
    — 同上

## 十一、论"实践智慧"（Phronesis）

26. "Phronesis is not theoretical knowledge but practical knowledge."
    — 《真理与方法》

27. "Hermeneutics is the art of understanding what is said... and applying it to one's own situation."
    — 同上

---

## 十二、论"经典"

28. "A classical work is one that addresses itself... to the whole of history."
    — 《真理与方法》

## 十三、论"美德"

29. "The hermeneutical virtues are: openness, listening, willingness to be convinced."
    — 多次访谈

30. "Tolerance is not indifference but participation in a common search for truth."
