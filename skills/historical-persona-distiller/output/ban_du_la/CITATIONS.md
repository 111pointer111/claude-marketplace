## 一、社会学习理论 / 观察学习的必要性

### C1. 观察学习的必要性（1977）
> "Learning would be exceedingly laborious, not to mention hazardous, if people had to rely solely on the effects of their own actions to inform them what to do."
**出处：** Social Learning Theory (1977) — 该书最著名开场
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 知识不仅来自直接经验（1977）
> "Fortunately, most human behavior is learned observationally through modeling: from observing others one forms an idea of how new behaviors are performed, and on later occasions this coded information serves as a guide for action."
**出处：** Social Learning Theory (1977) — 紧接 C1
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 2 条

## 二、Bobo Doll 实验与攻击行为研究

### C3. Bobo Doll 实验的直接结论（1961）
> "The findings of these experiments provide strong support for the hypothesis that the observation of filmed aggression can lower inhibitions against such behavior and that observation of non-aggressive models can instill inhibitory controls against aggression."
**出处：** Bandura, Ross & Ross (1961), Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 3 条

### C4. 榜样可以传递新行为（1961）
> "Modeling influences can produce novel behaviors that have not been in the model's repertoire, and they can transmit inhibitory and disinhibitory effects."
**出处：** Bandura, Ross & Ross (1961)
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 4 条

## 三、行为主义批判 / 把认知请回心理学

### C5. 行为主义放弃了它要解释的现象（1986）
> "Strict behaviorism, which precludes consideration of internal cognitions, excluded from its domain all activities mediated by cognition. In doing so, behaviorism forfeited the very phenomena it purported to explain."
**出处：** Social Foundations of Thought and Action (1986) — 序言
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 5 条

### C6. 心理学是行为与心智的科学（多年反复出现）
> "Psychology is the science of behavior and mind."
**出处：** 在多本著作中反复出现的领域定义，APA address 等多处使用

## 四、自我效能理论

### C7. 自我效能的定义（1997）
> "Self-efficacy is the belief in one's capabilities to organize and execute the courses of action required to manage prospective situations."
**出处：** Self-Efficacy: The Exercise of Control (1997), p. 3
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 7 条

### C8. 掌握性经验是最强来源（1997）
> "The most effective way of developing self-efficacy is through mastery experiences."
**出处：** Self-Efficacy: The Exercise of Control (1997)
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 8 条

### C9. 自我效能对行为的作用（1997）
> "Given the same environmental conditions, people who have a high sense of efficacy act differently from those who question their capabilities."
**出处：** Self-Efficacy: The Exercise of Control (1997)
**回溯：** raw/ban_du_la/引语.txt 第 9 条
