# 斯金纳 (B.F. Skinner) — 原文引用清单

---

## 一、行为主义哲学核心

### C1. 强化定义（1938）
> "The consequences of behavior determine the probability that the behavior will occur again."
**出处：** *The Behavior of Organisms* (1938), 引言
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 实验分析从单一个体开始（1938）
> "The experimental analysis of behavior… begins with the single organism."
**出处：** *The Behavior of Organisms* (1938)
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 2 条

### C3. 行为强度的操作化测量（1938）
> "The strength of a response is… measured in terms of the rate at which it is emitted."
**出处：** *The Behavior of Organisms* (1938)
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 3 条

## 二、条件反射与强化时间表

### C4. 强化时间表的研究传统
> Skinner 与 Charles Ferster 在 1957 *Schedules of Reinforcement* 中系统化了 FR（固定比率）/ VR（可变比率）/ FI（固定时距）/ VI（可变时距）四种基本时间表。
**出处：** Skinner & Ferster, *Schedules of Reinforcement* (1957)
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/传记.txt 第 5 条

### C5. 行为塑造（shaping）原则
> "We can strengthen a response by reinforcing closer and closer approximations to the final form."
**出处：** *The Behavior of Organisms* (1938) 与 *Walden Two* (1948) 教育章节中均有体现
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 4 条

---

## 三、教育哲学

### C6. 教育是遗忘后剩下的东西（1968）
> "Education is what survives when what has been learned has been forgotten."
**出处：** *The Technology of Teaching* (1968), 引言（常被误归皮亚杰）
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 5 条

### C7. 教育的最终目的（1972）
> "I am convinced that the ultimate aim of education is to enable people to enjoy the arts."
**出处：** 1972 Commencement Address, Western Kentucky University；亦见于 *The Technology of Teaching*
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 6 条

---

## 四、晚年哲学反思与自由/尊严

### C8. 自由不等于否认因果（1971）
> "The freedom of the individual is not to be sought in any denial of the causal nature of human behavior."
**出处：** *Beyond Freedom and Dignity* (1971), 第 3 章
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 7 条

### C9. 通过环境改变人类境况（多次再版引用）
> "We can change 'the human condition' by changing the environment."
**出处：** Skinner 多次演讲与论文集
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 8 条

## 五、机器与心智（被引用为先声）

### C10. 关于"思考"的真正问题
> "The real question is not whether machines think but whether men do."
**出处：** Skinner 多次演讲中被引用，被认知科学史视为 AI 心理学争论的先声
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/后世评述.txt 第 3 条

## 六、对手与论战

### C11. Chomsky 1959 对 Verbal Behavior 的著名批评
> Chomsky 1959 年发表 "A Review of B. F. Skinner's Verbal Behavior"（《语言》第 35 卷），认为 Skinner 的语言操作分析缺少对语言创造性和结构性的解释。
**出处：** Noam Chomsky, "A Review of B. F. Skinner's Verbal Behavior," *Language*, Vol. 35, No. 1 (1959), pp. 26-58
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/后世评述.txt 第 1 条

---

## 七、乌托邦愿景

### C12. 瓦尔登湖第二的行为工程学
> *Walden Two* (1948) 描绘了一个通过行为工程学设计的乌托邦社区：工作轮换、积极强化、民主决策、劳动价值重估。
**出处：** *Walden Two* (1948)
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/传记.txt 第 3 条

### C13. Skinner 对人本主义心理学的间接回应
> "I have since learned to detect a certain evasiveness in the popular distinction between the human and the merely animal."
**出处：** 引语流传；多见于 *About Behaviorism* (1974) 论述背景
**回溯：** raw/si_jin_na/引语.txt 第 9 条
