## 一、条件反射与高级神经活动

### C1. 条件反射研究起点（1903）
> "We have now the right to say, not that we have observed a psychical secretion, but that we have a true salivary reflex to the sound of a bell."
**出处：** Pavlov, "The Psychical Secretion of the Saliva" (1903 Madrid lecture, XIV International Medical Congress); reported in B. P. Babkin, *Pavlov: A Biography* (1949)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 条件反射的意义（1927）
> "The great importance of the conditioned reflex lies in the fact that it provides a physiology of the higher nervous activity."
**出处：** *Conditioned Reflexes: Lectures on the Physiology of Higher Nervous Activity* (1927), trans. W. Horsley Gantt (1928)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 2 条

### C3. 大脑两半球工作的核心（1927）
> "I want to reveal to you the workings of the most complex system of the universe—the cerebral hemispheres of the brain."
**出处：** *Lectures on the Work of the Cerebral Hemispheres* (1927)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 3 条

## 二、消化生理学（诺贝尔奖工作）

### C4. 消化腺研究的工作哲学（1897）
> "What I have presented are the answers of the digestive glands to the questions we have asked them, and as long as the question has been correctly put, the answers have always come."
**出处：** *Lektsii o rabote glavnykh pishchevaritel'nykh zhelez* / *The Work of the Digestive Glands* (1897), English trans. W. H. Thompson (1902)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 4 条

### C5. 诺贝尔奖授予理由（1904）
> "In recognition of his work on the physiology of digestion, through which knowledge in vital processes has been altered and extended in a remarkable way."
**出处：** Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 1904 — Prize Citation
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/后世评述.txt 第 1 条

## 三、科学方法与科学三原则

### C6. 事实是科学家的空气（1935）
> "Perfect as is the wing of a bird, it never could raise the bird up without resting on the air. Facts are the air of a scientist. Without them you never can fly. Without them your theories are vain efforts."
**出处：** *Letter to the Youth* / *Sovety i mysli Pavlova* (1935, advice to Soviet youth)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 5 条

### C7. 科学三原则：Learn, compare, collect the facts
> "Learn, compare, collect the facts!"
**出处：** attributed; widely cited in Pavlov biographical literature; the "Pavlov principle" of scientific method
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 6 条

### C8. 热爱工作（1910s, advice to students）
> "Science demands from a man all his life. If you had two lives that would not be enough for you. Be passionate about your work and your searches."
**出处：** attributed advice to students; recorded by B. P. Babkin in his biography (1949)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 7 条

## 四、生理学家与心理学家的关系

### C9. 生理学家不应取代心理学家（1910s）
> "When a physiologist is confronted with a problem of the soul, he must investigate it, but he will never replace a psychologist."
**出处：** attributed; recorded in Pavlov's various responses to psychology
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 8 条

### C10. 不要成为事实的奴隶（晚年）
> "Do not become the slave of your facts."
**出处：** attributed; part of Pavlov's later methodological reflections
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 9 条

## 五、晚年自我批判（1934）

### C11. 文化与历史因素（1934-06-28）
> "We have…wasting and unnecessary work…Both our thoughts and our actions are wholly dependent upon the cultural and historical conditions of our life."
**出处：** "Free Reflexes" speech, 28 June 1934, Leningrad Physiological Conference
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 10 条

### C12. 对纯生理化条件反射的反思（1934）
> "I think that the time has come to introduce a new element into our work—the cultural and historical element."
**出处：** Same source (28 June 1934 speech)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 11 条

## 六、科学与政府

### C13. 科学服务于工人阶级（1930）
> "As a scientist and citizen I should gladly put science at the service of my country and of the working class."
**出处：** Pavlov's 1930 statement, often quoted in Soviet propaganda and in critiques of his later stance
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/后世评述.txt 第 2 条

## 七、对手与论敌

### C14. 对行为主义者的边界界定（1920s）
> "Psychology is to physiology as the hand is to the body: it is its visible expression, but not its essence."
**出处：** attributed, Pavlov's responses to behaviourists; quoted by D. Todes in *Pavlov's Physiology Factory* (2002)
**回溯：** raw/ba_fu_luo_fu/引语.txt 第 12 条

### C15. 晚年学者的自白（1935）
> "I am old and I am full of years, but I am still learning."
**出处：** attributed, near end of life; recorded in Babkin biography
