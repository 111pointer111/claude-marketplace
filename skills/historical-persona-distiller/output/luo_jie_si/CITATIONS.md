# 卡尔·罗杰斯 (Carl Rogers) — 原文引用清单

---

## 一、来访者中心疗法（Client-Centered Therapy）

### C1. 来访者拥有自我实现的内在倾向（1961）
> "The organism has one basic tendency and striving — to actualize, maintain, and enhance the experiencing organism."
**出处：** On Becoming a Person (1961), Chapter 3 "Some Hypotheses Regarding the Facilitation of Personal Growth"
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 来访者能从关系中自我成长（1961）
> "If I can provide a certain type of relationship, the other person will discover within himself the capacity to use that relationship for growth, and change will occur."
**出处：** On Becoming a Person (1961)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 2 条

### C3. 好生活不是固定状态（1961）
> "I have gradually come to one negative conclusion about the good life. It seems to me that the good life is not any fixed state. It is not, as the term would suggest, a state of virtue, or happiness, or contentment."
**出处：** On Becoming a Person (1961)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 3 条

## 二、自我概念（Self-Concept）

### C4. 自我接纳与改变的悖论
> "The curious paradox is that when I accept myself just as I am, then I can change."
**出处：** On Becoming a Person (1961), also restated in Freedom to Learn (1969)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 4 条

### C5. 个人独特性的普遍意义
> "What is most personal is most universal."
**出处：** Carl Rogers on Encounter Groups (1970)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 5 条

---

## 三、充分条件（Necessary and Sufficient Conditions）

### C6. 共情是进入他人的知觉世界（1975）
> "Empathy is not merely a matter of feeling sympathetic; it is a matter of entering the private perceptual world of the other."
**出处：** "Empathic: An Unappreciated Way of Being" (1975), also restated in A Way of Being (1980)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 6 条

### C7. 真诚一致——治疗者必须真实（1961）
> "To be facilitated, the individual needs to be in a relationship in which the other person is real."
**出处：** On Becoming a Person (1961)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 7 条

### C8. 被理解与被接纳触发自我审视（ca. 1961）
> "In my experience, the more a person feels understood and accepted, the more they are willing to examine their own beliefs and behaviors."
**出处：** On Becoming a Person (1961), related formulation in "The Necessary and Sufficient Conditions" (1957)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 8 条

### C9. 真正被听见与理解的人才会真正改变（ca. 1961）
> "The only person who can really change himself is the person who is truly heard and understood."
**出处：** Restated formulation related to sufficient conditions, A Way of Being (1980)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 9 条

### C10. 多元与差异的价值（ca. 1970s）
> "If we value the individual and prize his or her uniqueness, then we must accept and even welcome the differences we see in others."
**出处：** Carl Rogers on Encounter Groups (1970); also restated in Freedom to Learn (1969/1983)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 10 条

## 四、Q 阶层（Q-Sort）与测量

### C11. 自我概念与理想自我的距离
Rogers 用 Q-sort 方法测量个体描述的"真实自我"（actual self）与"理想自我"（ideal self）之间的差距；差距越大，适应不良越严重。
**出处：** Client-Centered Therapy (1951), Chapter 6 "A Theory of Personality and Behavior"
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 2 条

### C12. 治疗中自我概念的改变
> "As a result of therapy, the individual becomes more congruent—his ideal self becomes more like his actual self."
**出处：** Related formulation, Client-Centered Therapy (1951), restated by subsequent researchers
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 3 条

## 五、教育（Education）

### C13. 真正受过教育的人能自我学习与改变（1969）
> "The only person who is educated is the one who has learned how to learn and change."
**出处：** Freedom to Learn (1969), Viewpoints Inc.
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 11 条

### C14. 教师是促进者，不是权威（1969）
Rogers 在 Freedom to Learn 中提出，教师应从"权威"转向"促进者"（facilitator），把学习的责任交还给学生。这一思想影响了全球人本主义教育运动。
**出处：** Freedom to Learn (1969)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/传记.txt 切片四节

## 六、晚年反思（A Way of Being, 1980）

### C15. 个人存在方式胜过理论体系
> "I find that the kind of person I am as I communicate to you is more important than anything I could say."
**出处：** Related formulation, A Way of Being (1980), also restated in Carl Rogers on Encounter Groups (1970)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/引语.txt 第 12 条

### C16. 自我作为一个过程
Rogers 在 1980 年代的演讲中明确反对把人格看作固定结构——他主张自我是一个"流动的过程"（a flowing process），由当下的体验组成。这是他晚年的存在主义化转向。
**出处：** A Way of Being (1980), Chapter 5 "The World of the Person-Centered Community"
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 5 条

---

## 七、对手（精神分析与行为主义）

### C17. 拒绝"诊断-病理"框架
Rogers 反对精神分析对来访者的标签化诊断——他主张不把来访者看作"病人"，而是看作"正在经历困难的人"。这是来访者中心疗法与传统精神分析的关键差异。
**出处：** Counseling and Psychotherapy (1942); Client-Centered Therapy (1951)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 7 条

### C18. 对行为主义的温和批评
Rogers 承认行为主义的实证精神，但他反对把人简化为 S-R 单元——他坚持人是有内在动力的有机体（organism），有其 actualizing tendency。
**出处：** Client-Centered Therapy (1951), Chapter 11; "Some Hypotheses Concerning the Facilitation of Personal Growth" (1947)
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 8 条

### C19. Maslow 与 Rogers 的不同
虽然 Maslow 与 Rogers 共同发起人本主义心理学第三势力，但两人取向不同：Maslow 偏存在主义（self-actualization 的层级模型），Rogers 偏临床（治疗关系与充分条件）。Rogers 的工作对临床训练的可操作影响更大。
**出处：** Maslow & Rogers 共著 "Synergy in the Society and the Individual" (1962); biographical accounts
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 9 条

## 八、后世评述

### C20. APA 学会调查：20 世纪最有影响力（1978）
> "Rogers was ranked as the second most influential psychotherapist by the American Psychological Association in a 1978 survey, alongside B. F. Skinner."
**出处：** APA historical survey; restated in Wikipedia and other biographical sources
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 1 条

### C21. Kirkpatrick 评论（1990s）
Rogers 是"在治疗中创造了'被理解'这个概念的先驱"——这是后人对他最简洁的总结。
**出处：** Kirkpatrick, L. A. (1993), textbook commentary, also various APA presidential speeches
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 4 条

### C22. 跨文化应用的成功
Rogers 与 Maria Bowen 在 1970s-80s 把以人为中心方法扩展到巴西、阿根廷、墨西哥、非洲、东亚等地，被认为是跨文化咨询的开山工作之一。
**出处：** Center for Studies of the Person archives; contemporary accounts of Bowen & Rogers
**回溯：** raw/luo_jie_si/后世评述.txt 第 6 条
