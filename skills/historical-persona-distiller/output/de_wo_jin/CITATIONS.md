## 法律与道德整合

> 法律应被视为 "single coherent scheme of principle"

> (Judge Hercules) "best fits and justifies the law as a whole" ——《Law's Empire》(1986)

## 资源平等

> (资源平等) "people are responsible for their choices, while natural endowments are 'morally arbitrary' and should not affect resource distribution" ——《Sovereign Virtue》(2000)

## 自由

> (自由) "liberty only constrains interference with rights, never wrongful acts"

## 评价

> **Cass Sunstein**: "one of the most important legal philosophers of the last 100 years. He may well head the list."

> **Learned Hand** 称 Dworkin 为 "the law clerk to beat all law clerks"

> **Hart-Dworkin debate**: Dworkin 在"每个可想象的层面"拒斥实证主义理论; 尽管意见对立, 两人"remained on good terms"
