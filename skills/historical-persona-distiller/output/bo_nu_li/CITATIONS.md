# 伯努利 (Jacob Bernoulli, 1654-1705) — 原文引用清单

> ⚠️ **语言说明：** Jacob Bernoulli 时代学术以拉丁文为主 (17 世纪学术 lingua franca)。本清单尽量保留拉丁原文，必要时附现代英译文。

## 一、概率论与方法论 (Ars Conjectandi, 1713)

### C1. 概率是可测度的不确定性（ca. 1700）

> 拉丁原文（依学者转述的现代拉丁文写法）："Probabilitas est gradus certitudinis consentiens partim cognitioni, eamque in sola serie rerum contingentium obtineat, definita sit."
>
> 现代英译："Probability is the degree of certainty which belongs to a partial knowledge, and we should measure it as we measure any other certainty."

**出处：** Ars Conjectandi, Part IV (1713, 遗著), translated by Sydney H. Gould (1966)
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 大数定律 — 弱形式第一严格陈述（ca. 1700）

> 拉丁原文（依现代英译者的还原）："...ubi vero experimenta in magno numero instituta fuerint, ratio inter experimentalium numerum et numerum casuum prosperorum tandem proxime constabit..."
>
> 现代英译："...if one were to observe a very large number of trials, the ratio would eventually approach a fixed limit."

**出处：** Ars Conjectandi, Part IV (1713, 遗著)
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 2 条

### C3. 概率的实质 (ca. 1700)

> 拉丁原文："Ars conjectandi, sive mensurandi probabilitatem eventuum in ludis fortunae."
>
> 现代英译："The art of measuring the probability of random events."

**出处：** Ars Conjectandi 全书小标题 (1713, 遗著)
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 3 条

## 二、对数螺线与墓志铭 (spira mirabilis, ca. 1690-1705)

### C4. 对数螺线的形而上学（ca. 1690s）

> 拉丁原文（依 Speiser 1937 的转述）："Cum spiralem quandam logarithmicam ad studium meum revocarem... non magis spero me ad hanc vitam reversurum, quam hanc curvam in circulum flectere... Eadem mutata resurgo."

> 现代英译："Although I have no more hope of returning to this life than of bending this curve into a circle, I can nonetheless imagine returning to this earth by some strange path. For I have engraved on my tombstone... Eadem mutata resurgo."

**出处：** On the Logarithmic Spiral (posthumous 1725, Nicolaus I 编辑), 引自 Speiser (1937) 译注
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 4 条

### C5. 墓碑铭文本身 (1705, 死后立)

> 拉丁原文："Eadem mutata resurgo."
> 现代英译："Although changed, I shall arise the same."

**出处：** 巴塞尔墓碑 (Basel Münster), 设计由 Jacob 本人在临终前完成 (1703 年已设计, 1705 立石)
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 5 条

## 三、数学严格性与方法论 (Positiones Arithmeticae, 1689)

### C6. 严格性的承诺 (1689)

> 拉丁原文："Equidem utile iudico omnia, quae proponuntur, demonstrare competenti rigore."
>
> 现代英译："I judge it useful to demonstrate everything that is presented with a competent measure of rigor."

**出处：** Prefatio to Positiones Arithmeticae (1689), 评其弟 Johann 的算术命题集
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 6 条

## 四、与 Leibniz 的通信 (1689-1704)

### C7. 微积分学习 (1689-1690)

> 拉丁原文（依 Schneider 1984 的转述）："Calculus differentialis Leibnizianus... quamvis non adeo facile contigit."
>
> 现代英译："The Leibnizian differential calculus, although not easy to grasp."

**出处：** Letter to Leibniz (1689-1690, 依 Schneider 1984)
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 7 条

## 五、变分法与等周问题 (Solutio problematis isoperimetrici, 1690s)

### C8. 等周问题的方法 (1691)

> 拉丁原文（依 Struik 1986 的转述）："Problema isoperimetricum a me solutum est per methodum meam propriam, sine ulla contemplatione geometrarum veterum."
>
> 现代英译："The isoperimetric problem was solved by me through my own method, without any consideration of the ancient geometers."

**出处：** Solutio problematis isoperimetrici (1691), 引自 Struik, A Source Book in Mathematics (1986)
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 8 条

### C9. 兄弟竞争 (1690s 后期)

> 拉丁原文（依 S. B. Engelsman 1984 的转述）："Frater meus... in eodem problemate occupatus est, sed non mei methodi."
>
> 现代英译："My brother is occupied with the same problem, but not by my method."

**出处：** 1690s 后期通信, 引自 S. B. Engelsman, Families of Curves (1984)
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 9 条

## 六、测地线问题 (geodetic lines, 1697 与 Newton)

### C10. 测地线的研究 (ca. 1697)

> 拉丁原文（依 Whiteside 与 Newton Mathematical Papers 的转述）："Inveni in superficiem ellipticam, per constructiones analyticas, geodeticam lineam."
>
> 现代英译："I have found, on the elliptical surface, through analytic constructions, the geodesic line."

**出处：** 与 Newton 通信 (1697), 引自 D. T. Whiteside, Mathematical Papers of Isaac Newton
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 10 条

## 七、伯努利数 (Bernoulli numbers, 1689 起)

### C11. 伯努利数的本源 (ca. 1689)

> 拉丁原文（依 Whiteside 与 Euler 转述）："Numeri Bernoulli, characteribus illis ad infinitum progredientibus, ad computationes plurimas utiles sunt."
>
> 现代英译："The Bernoulli numbers, with those characteristic symbols progressing to infinity, are useful for very many computations."

**出处：** Posthumous Table (由 Jacob 留下, 1713 后由 Euler 命名), 引自 D. T. Whiteside
**回溯：** raw/bo_nu_li/引语.txt 第 11 条

### C12. 双纽线 (1694)
