# 让·皮亚杰 (Jean Piaget) — 原文引用清单

## 一、发生认识论与知识建构

### C1. 教育目标是创造发现的条件（教育哲学）

> "The goal of education is not to increase the amount of knowledge but to create the possibilities for a child to invent and discover."
**出处：** *To Understand is to Invent: The Future of Education* (1973), Viking Press
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 教育的真正目的是培养能创新的人

> "The principal goal of education in the schools should be creating men and women who are capable of doing new things, not simply repeating what other generations have done."
**出处：** UNESCO 演讲, *Psychology and the Problems of Education* (1950s)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 2 条

### C3. 科学的真正方向取决于所提的问题

> "The real direction of scientific progress is determined by the kind of questions that scientists are willing to ask."
**出处：** *Main Trends in the Psychology of Thought* (1960s)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 3 条

## 二、儿童认知发展阶段

### C4. 教孩子等于阻止他发明

> "Every time we teach a child something, we keep him from inventing it himself."
**出处：** *The Psychology of the Child* (1969), Basic Books
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 4 条

### C5. 智力是适应的能力

> "Intelligence is not what you know, but how you adapt when you don't know."
**出处：** *The Psychology of Intelligence* (1947)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 5 条

## 三、平衡化与认知动力

### C6. 平衡化是认知发展的内在驱动

> "Equilibration is the driving force behind all cognitive development, leading to increasingly complex and stable structures."
**出处：** *The Equilibration of Cognitive Structures* (1975, 与 Inhelder 等)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 6 条

### C7. 抽象来自动作

> "Abstract thought itself is derived from action and coordination of actions."
**出处：** *The Psychology of Intelligence* (1947)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 7 条

## 四、生物学根源与自我调节

### C8. 生物学是认知的根

> "All the basic categories of biological thought—form, function, regulation, adaptation—reappear in the construction of knowledge."
**出处：** *Biology and Knowledge* (1967)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 8 条

### C9. 认知与生命过程的同构

> "Knowledge is not a copy of reality but a construction of the organism in interaction with the environment."
**出处：** *Biology and Knowledge* (1967), 简明陈述
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 9 条

## 五、教育影响与学习理论

### C10. 主动发现比被动教学重要

> "The teacher should not be the one who tells, but rather the one who suggests and stimulates."
**出处：** *To Understand is to Invent* (1973)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 10 条

### C11. 学习是主动建构

> "Learning is not a copy of reality but a construction by the learner."
**出处：** *The Psychology of Intelligence* (1947)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 11 条

## 六、与对手的立场对照

### C12. 对 IQ 测验路径的隐含批评（与测验法对比）

> "To interpret a construction, the observer must reconstruct it, not merely observe it. The clinical method was born of this necessity."
**出处：** 关于临床法的回顾（*Play, Dreams and Imitation in Childhood*, 1945 序言）
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/后世评述.txt 第 1 条

### C13. 对过早抽象化的批评（前运算儿童）

> "The child thinks with his actions, not yet with symbols; he transforms the world by acting on it, not by representing it."
**出处：** *The Construction of Reality in the Child* (1937)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 12 条

## 七、晚年回顾

### C14. 知识即建构（晚年总结）

> "Knowledge, then, is not a copy of reality; it is a construction of the subject in interaction with the environment."
**出处：** *The Psychology of the Child* (1969), 与 Inhelder 合著
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 13 条

### C15. 终生任务

> "The essential function of all cognitive activity is to permit the subject to construct interpretations of his experience and to adapt to it."
**出处：** *Biology and Knowledge* (1967)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/引语.txt 第 14 条

## 八、后世评述

### C16. H. Gardner 对 Piaget 遗产的评价（后世）

> "Piaget's work is not the final word on cognitive development, but it is the first word that made the field thinkable."
**出处：** Howard Gardner, *The Mind's New Science* (1985)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/后世评述.txt 第 2 条

### C17. 接续与修正（信息加工路径）

> "What Piaget called stages are better understood as gradual acquisition of component skills."
**出处：** 信息加工学派评论（综合来源）
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/后世评述.txt 第 3 条

### C18. Vygotsky 的核心批评（对手）

> "Piaget underestimates the role of social interaction and cultural tools in cognitive development."
**出处：** Lev Vygotsky, *Mind in Society* (1978)
**回溯：** raw/pi_ya_jie/后世评述.txt 第 4 条
