## 共同善

> discontent takes "the form of inchoate anxieties—a growing sense that we were losing control of the forces that govern our lives" ——《Democracy's Discontent》(1996)

## 精英统治

> "elites have been insulated from the accountability that a broader conception of the common good requires" ——《The Tyranny of Merit》(2020)

## 对 Rawls 的批判

> Rawls 的"无知之幕"把人们视为 "unencumbered selves"

## 课程

> *Justice* 课程是 "the university's first course to be made freely available online and on television", "tens of millions of people around the world"

## 中国影响

> *China Newsweek* 评为 2011 "most influential foreign figure of the year"

- "Communitarianism" (社群主义)
- "Common Good" (共同善)
- "Unencumbered Self" (无负担的自我)
- "Justice as Fairness vs Common Good" (作为公平的正义 vs 共同善)
- "Tyranny of Merit" (精英的暴政)
- "Moral Limits of Markets" (市场的道德边界)
