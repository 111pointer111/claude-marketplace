## 一、权力-知识（pouvoir-savoir）

1. "We should admit rather that power produces knowledge... power and knowledge directly imply one another; there is no power relation without the correlative constitution of a field of knowledge, nor any knowledge that does not presuppose and constitute at the same time power relations."
   — 《规训与惩罚》(1975) / Discipline and Punish, p. 27

2. "Power is everywhere: not that it engulfs everything, but that it comes from everywhere."
   — 《性史》卷一《认知的意志》(1976) / The History of Sexuality, Vol. 1

3. "In political thought and analysis, we still have not cut off the head of the king."
   — 《性史》卷一 (1976)

---

## 二、规训（discipline） / 全景敞视（panopticon）

4. "Discipline 'makes' individuals; it is the specific technique of a power that regards individuals both as objects and as instruments of its exercise."
   — 《规训与惩罚》(1975)

5. "He who is subjected to a field of visibility, and who knows it, assumes responsibility for the constraints of power; he makes them play spontaneously upon himself; he inscribes in himself the power relation in which he simultaneously plays both roles; he becomes the principle of his own subjection."
   — 《规训与惩罚》(1975) / p. 202-203

6. "Is it surprising that prisons resemble factories, schools, barracks, hospitals, which all resemble prisons?"
   — 《规训与惩罚》(1975)

---

## 三、坦白 / 忏悔（confession）

7. "Western man has become a confessing animal."
   — 《性史》卷一 (1976) / The Will to Knowledge, p. 59

8. "The most defenseless tenderness and the bloodliest of powers have a similar need of confession. Western man has become a confessing animal."
   — 同上

9. "A real subjection is born mechanically from a fictitious relation."
   — 同上

## 四、生命权力 / 治理术

10. "For millennia, the sovereign's right was the right to take life or let live. Then came a time when power was no longer exercised primarily as a right to take life; instead, it began to take life as its object and to exercise upon it a power bent on generating, controlling, and regulating it."
    — 《性史》卷一 (1976) / p. 138

11. "The judges of normality are present everywhere. We are in the society of the teacher-judge, the doctor-judge, the educator-judge, the social-worker-judge."
    — 同上

12. "Our society is one not of spectacle, but of surveillance."
    — 同上

13. "the new technologies of power address the population"
    — 《法兰西学院课程》(1977-1978) / "Security, Territory, Population"

## 五、主体与自我技术

14. "Do not ask who I am and do not ask me to remain the same."
    — 《知识考古学》(1969) / The Archaeology of Knowledge, preface

15. "Maybe the target nowadays is not to discover what we are but to refuse what we are."
    — 《性史》卷一(1976)

16. "I have been trying... to see how the subject constitutes itself as subject through practices of the self."
    — 1984 伯克利讲座（身后整理 2011）

17. "the care of the self... is, in a sense, the opposite of the Cartesian cogito."
    — 《性史》卷三《自我的关切》(1984) / The Care of the Self

18. "Parrhesia... the parrhesiastes is the one who speaks the truth."
    — 1983 伯克利讲座 "Discourse and Truth"（身后整理 2019）/ The Courage of Truth

## 六、考古学 / 谱系学

19. "Genealogy does not pretend to go back in time to restore an unbroken continuity... its duty is to respect the singularity of events."
    — 《尼采、谱系学、历史》(1971) / Nietzsche, Genealogy, History

20. "Where the soul pretends unity, the genealogist sets out to notice the body's multiple and divided systems."
    — 同上

21. "in every society the production of discourse is at once controlled, selected, organised and redistributed."
    — 《话语的秩序》(1970) / The Discourse on Language (就职演讲)

## 七、人之死 / 知识型

22. "Man is an invention of recent date. And one perhaps nearing its end."
    — 《词与物》(1966) / The Order of Things, ending

23. "Marxism exists in nineteenth-century thought as a fish exists in water; that is, it is unable to breathe anywhere else."
    — 同上

24. "The gesture that divides madness is the constitutive one."
    — 《疯癫与非理性》(1961) / Madness and Civilization

25. "Unreason is to reason as dazzlement is to daylight."
    — 同上
