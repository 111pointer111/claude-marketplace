## 证实主义 (Verificationism)

> "the meaning of every proposition is exhaustively determined by its verification of the given" ——《Positivism and Realism》(1932-33)

> "consistent empiricism" (Schlick 自定位)

## 拒斥形而上学

> "synonymous with meaningless" ——《Allgemeine Erkenntnislehre》(1918/1925, 论形而上学)

> "solipsism is a contingent truth which is treated by its defenders as unfalsifiable" ——《Meaning and Verification》(1936)

## 自由意志

> "Universal validity is something altogether different from 'compulsion'."

## 伦理学 (eudemonism)

> "Increase your happiness!" ——《Problems of Ethics》(1930)

## 维特根斯坦解读

> (哲学) "the alpha and omega of all scientific knowledge"

## 维也纳学派宣言

> "The scientific world-conception serves life, and life receives it." ——《Vienna Circle Manifesto》(1929, 与 Carnap/Hahn/Neurath 合著)

## 同时代人评价

> **Feigl and Blumberg**: "No other thinker was so well prepared to give new impetus to the philosophical questings of the younger generation."

> **Einstein**: 赞 *Raum und Zeit in der gegenwärtigen Physik* (1917) 的清晰性
