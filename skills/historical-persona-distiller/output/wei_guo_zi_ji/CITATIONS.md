## 一、文化历史理论 (Cultural-Historical Theory)

### C1. 内化原则（1934 / 1978 英文版）
> "Every function in the child's cultural development appears twice: first, on the social level, and later, on the individual level; first, between people (interpsychological), and then inside the child (intrapsychological)."
**出处：** Mind in Society (1978, Harvard University Press), p. 57 — Cole, John-Steiner, Scribner, Souberman 编
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 1 条

### C2. 高级心理机能的社会起源
> "The history of every higher mental function is external, in that it is social; only later does it become internal, in that it is an individual property."
**出处：** The Collected Works of L.S. Vygotsky, Vol. 4 (1997, Plenum), original 1930s manuscripts
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 2 条

## 二、最近发展区 (Zone of Proximal Development, ZPD)

### C3. ZPD 定义（1934 / 1978 英文版）
> "The zone of proximal development defines those functions that have not yet matured but are in a state of emerging maturation."
**出处：** Mind in Society (1978), p. 86
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 3 条

### C4. ZPD 的距离定义（1934 / 1978 英文版）
> "The distance between the actual developmental level as determined by independent problem solving and the level of potential development as determined through problem solving under adult guidance, or in collaboration with more capable peers."
**出处：** Mind in Society (1978), p. 86
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 4 条

## 三、中介理论 (Mediation)

### C5. 符号中介的中介（1934 / 1978 英文版）
> "The child begins to perceive the world not only through his eyes, but also through his speech."
**出处：** Thought and Language (1934 / 1962 MIT Press 英文版), Chapter 5
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 5 条

### C6. 心理工具作为中介
> "By using signs, the child acquires the ability to control his own psychological processes."
**出处：** Mind in Society (1978), Chapter 3
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 6 条

## 四、思维与言语 (Thought and Language)

### C7. 思维与言语是异质的（1934 / 1962 英文版）
> "Thought and speech are not the same thing, nor are they in any sense identical."
**出处：** Thought and Language (1934, Russian original / 1962 MIT Press English translation by Kozulin), Chapter 1
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 7 条

### C8. 通过他人成为自己
> "Through other people we become ourselves."
**出处：** paraphrase widely attributed to Vygotsky's general thesis in Mind in Society and Thought and Language
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 8 条

## 五、缺陷学 (Defektologiya)

### C9. 反对生物决定论（1929-1934 / 1983 出版）
> "如果一个盲童的死亡悲剧是不可避免的，他的死亡就是走向现实的胜利。"
**出处：** 《缺陷学基础》(Foundations of Defectology, 1929-1934 论文集，1983 出版)
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 9 条

### C10. 残障儿童的社会文化可改变性
> "缺陷学的基本命题：身体缺陷本身并不是儿童发展不足的最终原因；社会后果才是关键。"
**出处：** 《缺陷学基础》 概述
**回溯：** raw/wei_guo_zi_ji/引语.txt 第 10 条
