## 一、论"原初立场"（Original Position）

1. "The original position is, I think, the most natural and most general way to make the principles of justice explicit."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §3

2. "The parties in the original position are not... self-interested in any straightforward sense."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §25

3. "The principles of justice are chosen behind a veil of ignorance."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §3

---

## 二、论"无知之幕"（Veil of Ignorance）

4. "The veil of ignorance prevents us from... using the particular contingencies of our own position to strike a self-interested bargain."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §4

5. "No one knows his place in society, his natural assets, his conception of the good, or the particular circumstances of his own society."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §24

---

## 三、论"两个正义原则"

6. "First: each person is to have an equal right to the most extensive basic liberty compatible with a similar liberty for others."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §11

7. "Second: social and economic inequalities are to satisfy two conditions: (a) they are to be attached to offices and positions open to all under conditions of fair equality of opportunity; and (b) they are to be to the greatest benefit of the least advantaged members of society."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §11

8. "The difference principle... is, in effect, an agreement to share the benefits of natural talents."
   — *A Theory of Justice*, §17

---

## 四、论"作为公平的正义"

9. "Justice as fairness... begins with the idea that society should be a fair system of cooperation."
   — *Political Liberalism*, lecture I

10. "A society is well-ordered when it is designed to advance the good of its members and is effectively regulated by a public conception of justice."
    — *A Theory of Justice*, §1

---

## 五、论"政治自由主义"

11. "Political liberalism... regards the political order as a self-standing domain."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture III

12. "An overlapping consensus... consists of all the reasonable opposing religious, philosophical, and moral doctrines likely to endure over generations."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture IV

13. "Public reason... is the reason of equal citizens."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture VI

14. "Justice as fairness does not... claim to be true in a comprehensive sense."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture IV

## 六、论"善"

15. "The good is the satisfaction of rational desire."
    — *A Theory of Justice*, §15

16. "A person's good is determined by what is for him the most rational long-term plan."
    — *A Theory of Justice*, §63

---

## 七、论"反思平衡"

17. "Reflective equilibrium is the state of coherence between our considered judgments and the principles we have adopted."
    — *A Theory of Justice*, §4 / *Political Liberalism*

18. "We can revise our considered judgments... when the principles we have tentatively proposed do not fit them."
    — *A Theory of Justice*, §4

---

## 八、论"康德式建构主义"

19. "Justice as fairness is constructivist... its principles are the result of a process of construction."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture III

20. "Kantian constructivism holds that moral principles are the result of a practical reason's procedure of construction."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture III

## 九、论"基本结构"

21. "The basic structure of society is the way in which the main political and social institutions distribute fundamental rights and duties."
    — *A Theory of Justice*, §2

## 十、论"公民身份"

22. "Citizens are free and equal persons."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture III

23. "They have two moral powers: a capacity for a sense of justice and a capacity for a conception of the good."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture III

24. "We are not... self-sufficient individuals who enter into society to advance our interests."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture IV

25. "The self is prior to the ends which are affirmed by it."
    — *A Theory of Justice*, §61

## 十一、论"宽容"

26. "Toleration is not indifference but... respect for others as equals."
    — *Political Liberalism*

## 十二、论"正义感"

27. "A sense of justice is a willingness to share in fair terms of cooperation."
    — *Political Liberalism*, lecture VI

## 十三、论"哲学方法"

28. "The aim of political philosophy, when things go well, is to bring our considered judgments into reflective equilibrium."
