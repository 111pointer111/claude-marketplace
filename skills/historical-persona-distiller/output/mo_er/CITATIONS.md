## 反唯心论 (Refutation of Idealism)

> "when we try to introspect the sensation of blue, all we can see is the blue" ——《The Nature of Judgment》(1899) / 《Refutation of Idealism》(1903)

> "'blue' is as much an object… as the most exalted and independent real thing" ——《Refutation of Idealism》(1903)

> "it is indifferent to their nature whether anybody thinks them or not" ——《The Nature of Judgment》(1899)

## 真理 (Truth, 1901-02)

> "a truth differs in no respect from the reality to which it was supposed merely to correspond" ——《Truth》(1901-02)

> "That 'to be true' means to be thought in a certain way is, therefore, certainly false." ——《Principia Ethica》(1903) p. 183

## 元伦理学 (Principia Ethica, 1903)

> "these properties, in fact, were simply not 'other,' but absolutely and entirely the same with goodness." ——《Principia Ethica》§10

> "The value of a whole must not be assumed to be the same as the sum of the values of its parts." ——《Principia Ethica》§18

> "Right actions are those producing the most good." ——《Principia Ethica》

## 常识哲学 (A Defence of Common Sense, 1925)

> "the earth had existed also for many years before my body was born"

> "There exists at present a living human body, which is my body."

## 外在世界 (Proof of an External World, 1939)

> "Here is one hand… and here is another"

> "How? By holding up my two hands" ——《Proof of an External World》p. 166

> "How absurd it would be to suggest that I did not know it, but only believed it"

## Moore 悖论 (Moore's Paradox)

> "It is raining, but I do not believe it is raining"
