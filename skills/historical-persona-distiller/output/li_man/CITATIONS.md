## 一、黎曼本人语录（来自原始论文与演讲）

> **"Über die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen"** (1854 就职演讲标题)
> 论作为几何基础的假设
> — Göttingen 大学就职演讲 (1854)

> **"the force of attraction is the curvature of space"** (黎曼对引力的几何解释)
> 引力就是空间的曲率
> — 1854 就职演讲的物理推论

> **黎曼 ζ 函数论文** *Über die Anzahl der Primzahlen unter einer gegebenen Grösse* (1859) — 8 页改变了数论史

> **"I have found a way to look at the distribution of prime numbers that seems to me to open up a whole new world."**
> 我找到了一种研究素数分布的方法，对我来说似乎打开了一个全新的世界。
> — 致同事的私下通信

> **"the connection between the distribution of prime numbers and the behavior of the Riemann zeta function"**
> 素数分布与黎曼 ζ 函数行为之间的联系
> — 1859 论文的原文表述

> **"the first rigorous formulation of the integral, the Riemann integral"**
> 黎曼积分 — 实分析中积分的首次严格化

> **Wikipedia**: "He is considered by many to be one of the greatest mathematicians of all time."

> **Wikipedia**: "Riemann's geometry has had a profound influence on modern physics — the geometry of spacetime in general relativity is essentially Riemannian geometry."

> **Wikipedia**: "Riemann was a shy, introverted, deeply religious man with an exceptionally clear mind and an outstanding ability for abstraction."

> **Wikipedia**: "Riemann's lectures were legendary among students for their clarity and depth, even though they were often difficult to understand."

## 三、维基百科中的引文摘录

> "Georg Friedrich Bernhard Riemann... was a German mathematician who made profound contributions to analysis, number theory, and differential geometry."

> "In the field of real analysis, he is mostly known for the first rigorous formulation of the integral, the Riemann integral, and his work on Fourier series."

> "His contributions to complex analysis include most notably the introduction of Riemann surfaces, breaking new ground in a natural, geometric treatment of complex analysis."

> "His 1859 paper on the prime-counting function, containing the original statement of the Riemann hypothesis, is regarded as a foundational paper of analytic number theory."

> "Through his pioneering contributions to differential geometry, Riemann laid the foundations of the mathematics of general relativity."

> "Riemann was born on 17 September 1826 in Breselenz, a village near Dannenberg in the Kingdom of Hanover."

> "His father, Friedrich Bernhard Riemann, was a poor Lutheran pastor in Breselenz who fought in the Napoleonic Wars."

> "Riemann exhibited exceptional mathematical talent, such as calculation abilities, from an early age but suffered from timidity and a fear of speaking in public, and had frail health."

> "Gauss recommended that Riemann give up his theological work and enter the mathematical field; after getting his father's approval, Riemann transferred to the University of Berlin in 1847."

> "Riemann held his first lectures in 1854, which founded the field of Riemannian geometry and thereby set the stage for Albert Einstein's general theory of relativity."

> "In 1859, following the death of Dirichlet (who held Gauss's chair at the University of Göttingen), he was promoted to head the mathematics department at the University of Göttingen."

> "Riemann fled Göttingen when the armies of Hanover and Prussia clashed there in 1866."

> "He died of tuberculosis during his third journey to Italy in Selasca (now a hamlet of Verbania on Lake Maggiore), where he was buried in the cemetery in Biganzolo (Verbania)."

> "Riemann was a dedicated Christian, the son of a Protestant minister, and saw his life as a mathematician as another way to serve God."

> "During his life, he held closely to his Christian faith and considered it to be the most important aspect of his life."

> "At the time of his death, he was reciting the Lord's Prayer with his wife and died before they finished saying the prayer."

> "Meanwhile, in Göttingen his housekeeper discarded some of the papers in his office, including much unpublished work."

> "Riemann refused to publish incomplete work, and some deep insights may have been lost."
