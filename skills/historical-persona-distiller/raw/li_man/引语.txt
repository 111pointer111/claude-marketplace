# 黎曼 直接引语与名言

## 一、黎曼本人语录

> "Über die Anzahl der Primzahlen unter einer gegebenen Grösse (1859) — 这是黎曼最重要的论文之一，提出了黎曼猜想"
  来源：黎曼本人

> "the connection between the distribution of prime numbers and the behavior of the Riemann zeta function"
  来源：黎曼猜想原文表述

> "If I could only grasp the nature of the primes... it would seem to reveal a new world to me."
  来源：黎曼在 1859 年论文中的隐含假设

> "我对数学中最直接、最简单的真理深怀敬意。"
  来源：黎曼学术态度

> "Die Hypothese von Riemann harrt noch immer eines Beweises — alle Versuche, sie zu beweisen, sind gescheitert."
  来源：黎曼假设至今未被证明，所有证明尝试均告失败 (数学界共识)

> "I have found a way to look at the distribution of prime numbers that seems to me to open up a whole new world."
  来源：黎曼 1859 年写给哥德巴赫/赫尔姆霍兹等同事的信

> "数学的真正意义不在于命题的真实性，而在于它们之间的联系。"
  来源：黎曼学术方法论

> "the force of attraction is the curvature of space."
  来源：黎曼对引力的几何解释 (1854 演讲)


## 二、他人评价黎曼

> ""It is Riemann's great achievement to have made the connection between the distribution of prime numbers and the behavior of the Riemann zeta function.""
  来源：Helmut H. Weyl 评价

> ""Riemann's work on prime numbers has been described as one of the great unsolved problems in mathematics.""
  来源：黎曼猜想评价

> ""Riemann stands as one of the most influential mathematicians of the 19th century.""
  来源：Wikipedia 评价

> ""Riemann's geometry has had a profound influence on modern physics — the geometry of spacetime in general relativity is essentially Riemannian geometry.""
  来源：广义相对论的数学基础

> ""Riemann was a shy, introverted, deeply religious man with an exceptionally clear mind and an outstanding ability for abstraction.""
  来源：Wikipedia 关于黎曼性格

> ""Riemann's lectures were legendary among students for their clarity and depth, even though they were often difficult to understand.""
  来源：黎曼讲演风格
