# 拉格朗日 直接引语与名言

## 一、拉格朗日本人语录

> "The reader will find no figures in this work. The methods which I set forth do not require either geometrical or mechanical, but only algebraic, operations."
  来源：《分析力学》序言 (1788)

> "Newton was the greatest genius that ever existed, but the most fortunate; for we cannot find more than once a system of the world to establish."
  来源：拉格朗日评牛顿

> "I do not know whether I should call this a new method or a particular case of the method of variations."
  来源：变分法早期论文

> "As long as algebra and geometry have been separated, their progress has been slow and their uses limited; but when these two sciences were united, they gave each other mutual support and advanced rapidly together."
  来源：关于解析与几何结合

> "It seems to me that the most important advancement in mathematics has come from the union of algebra and analysis with geometry."
  来源：拉格朗日对数学综合的看法

> "The mathematical sciences present the most brilliant example of how pure reason may successfully enlarge the domain of knowledge without the help of experience."
  来源：拉格朗日对纯数学的看法

> "Mécanique analytique — a new science, treating of the equilibrium and motion of bodies by means of calculations."
  来源：拉格朗日对分析力学的定义

> "Such a simplification of mechanics, in which all the principles are reduced to a single variational principle, is a beautiful result."
  来源：拉格朗日对最小作用量原理的评价
