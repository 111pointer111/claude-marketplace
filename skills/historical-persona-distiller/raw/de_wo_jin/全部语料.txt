# 一、传记

## 基本信息
- **Ronald Dworkin** (1931-12-11 至 2013-02-14)
- 美国法律哲学家, 法理学与政治理论的桥梁

## 教育
- 1953 Harvard A.B., summa cum laude, 哲学
- Rhodes Scholar at Magdalen College, Oxford
- 1957 Harvard Law School J.D., magna cum laude

## 学术生涯
- Hand 法官书记员
- 1962 入 Yale Law School
- **1969 继 H.L.A. Hart 任 Oxford 法理学教授**
- 后任 UCL Quain / Bentham Professor
- NYU Law School Frank Henry Sommer 教授

## 核心思想
1. 法律作为规则与原则 — 拒斥法律实证主义
2. 正确答案论 — Judge Hercules
3. 宪法的道德解读
4. 平等理论 — 资源平等
5. 积极与消极自由
6. 解释主义 / 法律的整体性 (Law as Integrity)

## 主要著作
- *Taking Rights Seriously* (1977)
- *A Matter of Principle* (1985)
- *Law's Empire* (1986)
- *Sovereign Virtue* (2000)
- *Justice for Hedgehogs* (2011)
- *Religion Without God* (2013)

## Hart-Dworkin 辩论
- 与 H.L.A. Hart 的核心辩论
- Dworkin 在"每个可想象的层面"拒斥实证主义

# 二、直接引语

- 法律应被视为 "single coherent scheme of principle"
- (Judge Hercules) "best fits and justifies the law as a whole"
- (资源平等) "people are responsible for their choices, while natural endowments are 'morally arbitrary' and should not affect resource distribution"
- (自由) "liberty only constrains interference with rights, never wrongful acts"
- Cass Sunstein: "one of the most important legal philosophers of the last 100 years. He may well head the list."
- Learned Hand 称 Dworkin 为 "the law clerk to beat all law clerks"

# 三、后世评述

- 20 世纪最重要的法律哲学家之一
- 把法理学与政治理论 / 道德哲学整合
- *Law's Empire* 是 20 世纪法理学经典
- Dworkin-Hart 辩论是 20 世纪法理学最重要辩论之一
- *Taking Rights Seriously* 影响美国宪法理论
- Balzan Prize (2012), Holberg (2007) 等
