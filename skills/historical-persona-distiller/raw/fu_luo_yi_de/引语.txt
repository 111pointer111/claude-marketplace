# 弗洛伊德 直接引语与名言

## 一、弗洛伊德本人语录

> "The interpretation of dreams is the royal road to a knowledge of the unconscious activities of the mind."
  来源：《梦的解析》(1900)

> "Where id was, there ego shall be."
  来源：关于自我与本我

> "The ego is not master in its own house."
  来源：关于自我的局限

> "Being entirely honest with oneself is a good exercise."
  来源：自我分析

> "The voice of the intellect is a soft one, but it does not rest until it has gained a hearing."
  来源：《幻想的未来》(1927)

> "Most people do not really want freedom, because freedom involves responsibility, and most people are frightened of responsibility."
  来源：关于自由

> "Civilization is something which is imposed on the majority by those few who understand the value of it."
  来源：《文明及其不满》(1930)

> "The first requisite of education is the capacity to do without it."
  来源：关于教育

> "The great question that has never been answered, and which I have not yet been able to answer, despite my thirty years of research into the feminine soul, is 'What does a woman want?'"
  来源：关于女性 (致 Marie Bonaparte)

> "We are never so defenseless against suffering as when we love."
  来源：《文明及其不满》

> "Time passes slowly when you are alone with your thoughts."
  来源：与 Einstein 的通信 (1932)

> "Illusion of freedom is one of the deepest illusions of human nature."
  来源：关于自由意志

> "The mind is like an iceberg, it floats with one-seventh of its bulk above water."
  来源：关于潜意识

> "Dreams are the royal road to the unconscious."
  来源：《梦的解析》

> "The interpretation of dreams is the royal road to a knowledge of the unconscious activities of the mind."
  来源：《梦的解析》开篇

> "What we call happiness does not come from without; it is the satisfaction of the libido that has been repressed."
  来源：关于幸福

> "The unconscious mind is the source of all human behavior."
  来源：关于潜意识
