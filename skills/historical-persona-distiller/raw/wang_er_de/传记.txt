# 王尔德 传记

> 来源: Wikipedia
> 抓取日期: 2026-07-26

## 基本信息
- **王尔德** (Oscar Fingal O'Fflahertie Wills Wilde, 1854-10-16 至 1900-11-30)
- 爱尔兰作家/诗人/剧作家

## 关键年表
- 1854 生于都柏林 (Sir William Wilde + Jane Wilde 之 子)
- 1871-74 都柏林三一学院 (Royal School 奖学金)
- **1874-78 牛津 Magdalen College** (Greats 学位)
- 1878 获 Newdigate Prize "Ravenna"
- 1881 《诗集》出版
- 1882 北美讲学 "The English Renaissance"
- 1884 与 Constance Lloyd 结婚; 两子 (Cyril, Vyvyan)
- 1887-89 编辑《妇女世界》
- 1888 《快乐王子与其他故事》
- **1890 《道连·格雷画像》** — 唯一长篇
- 1891 annus mirabilis;《意向集》(Intention)
- 1892 《温德米尔夫人的扇子》
- 1893 《无足轻重的女人》;《莎乐美》用法语写
- **1895 《不可儿戏》《理想丈夫》;犯"严重猥亵罪"入狱 1895-97**
- 1897 出狱;狱中写《De Profundis》
- 1898 《雷丁监狱之歌》
- **1900 巴黎去世;葬于拉雪兹神父公墓**

## 关键引语
> "Life imitates art far more than art imitates life."
> "Art is individualism, and individualism is a disturbing and disintegrating force."
> "Man is least himself when he talks in his own person. Give him a mask, and he will tell you the truth."
> "I find it harder and harder every day to live up to my blue china."
> "There is only one thing in the world worse than being talked about, and that is not being talked about."

## 遗产
- 维多利亚时代晚期 唯美主义/颓废派 关键人物
- 以机智锋利、华丽服饰、精彩对话闻名
- "gross indecency" 罪定罪后入狱, 流亡
- 2017 身后获平反
