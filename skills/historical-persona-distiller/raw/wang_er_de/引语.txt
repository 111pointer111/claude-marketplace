# Oscar Wilde — 直接引语清单

来源：Biography.com + Wikiquote + Wikipedia

---

## 关于艺术与生活

1. "Life imitates art far more than art imitates life." — The Decay of Lying (1891)
2. "Art is individualism, and individualism is a disturbing and disintegrating force." — The Soul of Man under Socialism (1891)
3. "There is no such thing as a moral or an immoral book. Books are well written, or badly written." — The Picture of Dorian Gray preface (1890)

## 关于自我与面具

4. "Man is least himself when he talks in his own person. Give him a mask, and he will tell you the truth." — Intentions (1891)
5. "I find it harder and harder every day to live up to my blue china." — attributed
6. "Be yourself; everyone else is already taken." — attributed

## 关于名声

7. "There is only one thing in the world worse than being talked about, and that is not being talked about." — The Importance of Being Earnest (1895)
8. "The only thing worse than being talked about is not being talked about." — 同上

## 关于美与体验

9. "I can resist everything except temptation." — Lady Windermere's Fan (1892)
10. "We are all in the gutter, but some of us are looking at the stars." — Lady Windermere's Fan (1892)
11. "A man's face is his autobiography. A man's face is a work in progress." — attributed

## 关于经验与痛苦

12. "Experience is merely the name men give to their mistakes." — The Importance of Being Earnest (1895)
13. "Suffering is one very long moment. We cannot divide it by seasons." — De Profundis (1897)

## 关于爱与牺牲

14. "The only way to get rid of temptation is to yield to it." — The Picture of Dorian Gray (1890)
15. "Behind joy and laughter there may be a temperament, a sweet doubleness." — Intentions (1891)

## 关于社会与习俗

16. "Fashion is a form of ugliness so intolerable that we have to alter it every six months." — attributed
17. "A true friend stabs you in the front." — attributed
18. "Always forgive your enemies — nothing annoys them so much." — attributed
19. "The truth is rarely pure and never simple." — The Importance of Being Earnest (1895)
20. "Memory… is the diary that we all carry about with us." — The Importance of Being Earnest (1895)

## 《雷丁监狱之歌》

21. "Yet each man kills the thing he loves / By each let this be heard / Some do it with a bitter look / Some with a flattering word." — The Ballad of Reading Gaol (1898)
