# 伽达默尔 (Hans-Georg Gadamer) — 直接引语清单

> 来源：Wikipedia、SEP、IEP 中所引伽达默尔原文，及其著作已知关键段落
> 抓取日期：2026-07-19

---

## 一、论"偏见"（Vorurteil）

1. "Prejudices are not simply obstacles to understanding but conditions of understanding."
   — 《真理与方法》(1960) / Truth and Method, p. 278
   — 中译：偏见不仅是理解的障碍，而且是理解的条件。
   — 反驳启蒙运动把"偏见"贬为"非理性残余"的传统。

2. "The Enlightenment's critique of prejudice... is itself the prejudice of the Enlightenment."
   — 同上
   — 启蒙对偏见的批判本身就是一种偏见（反对偏见的偏见）。

---

## 二、论"视域融合"

3. "The horizon of the present cannot be formed without the past. There is no more an isolated horizon of the present than there are timeless horizons."
   — 《真理与方法》p. 304
   — 现在的视域不能脱离过去而形成。

4. "Understanding is always the fusion of these horizons which we imagine to exist by themselves."
   — 同上
   — 理解始终是这些视域的融合，而这些视域是我们想象独立存在的。

---

## 三、论"效果历史意识"

5. "Consciousness of being affected by history (wirkungsgeschichtliches Bewusstsein) is first of all consciousness of the hermeneutical situation."
   — 《真理与方法》p. 311
   — 效果历史意识首先是对解释学处境的意识。

6. "The historical nature of understanding... is not something we can overcome or put aside."
   — 同上

---

## 四、论"应用"（Anwendung）

7. "Application is not a separate part of the phenomenon of understanding... it is the heart of the problem of hermeneutics."
   — 《真理与方法》
   — 应用不是理解现象的独立部分，而是解释学问题的核心。

8. "Understanding is always application."
   — 同上 / Truth and Method, p. 324

---

## 五、论"对话"

9. "Reaching an understanding... is always a process of coming to an understanding through conversation."
   — 《真理与方法》

10. "To reach an understanding in a dialogue means... to be transformed through the encounter with the other."
    — 《真理与方法》
    — 在对话中达成理解，意味着通过与他者的相遇而被转化。

11. "The horizon of the other... must be understood as a possibility of one's own being."
    — 同上
    — 他者的视域必须被理解为自身存在的可能性。

---

## 六、论"游戏"（Spiel）

12. "Play is not the player's subjective attitude but the movement itself that is play."
    — 《真理与方法》/ p. 102
    — 游戏不是玩家的主观态度，而是游戏本身的运动。

13. "The work of art itself is the play that it plays."
    — 同上
    — 艺术作品本身就是它所玩的游戏。

---

## 七、论"效果历史"

14. "To think historically means... to be conscious of one's own historical situatedness."
    — 《真理与方法》/ p. 311

15. "There is no statement that is not motivated."
    — 《真理与方法》/ p. 358
    — 没有无动机的陈述——所有理解都预设了动机/语境。

---

## 八、论"传统"

16. "Tradition is not simply a process that we experience but a language that we speak."
    — 《真理与方法》
    — 传统不只是我们经历的过程，而是我们说的语言。

17. "The authority of tradition... has its legitimation in the fact that it preserves what would otherwise be lost."
    — 同上

---

## 九、论"自我反思"

18. "I had the terrible feeling that Heidegger was standing behind me and looking over my shoulder."
    — Gadamer 1997b, 15 (Reflections on my Philosophical Journey)
    — 我有一种可怕的感觉，海德格尔就站在我身后，看着我的肩膀。
    — 1997 年自传性反思，体现伽达默尔对海德格尔"压力"而非"模板"的体验。

19. "I have never claimed to be a Heideggerian."
    — 多次访谈中明示
    — 我从未声称自己是海德格尔主义者。

20. "Without [Heidegger] I would not have said a single word."
    — 多次明示
    — 没有他我说不出一句话——但这不等于"我是他的门徒"。

---

## 十、论"教育"

21. "Education is not something that someone else does to us but a process of self-formation."
    — 《真理与方法》

22. "Sensus communis is the community of feeling... not just intellectual agreement but a shared sense of what is right."
    — 《真理与方法》

---

## 十一、论"美与艺术"

23. "The work of art is not an object that stands over against a subject for itself."
    — 《真理与方法》/ p. 102
    — 艺术作品不是独立于主体而存在的对象。

24. "Truth is not merely what is methodologically certain but what illuminates and reveals."
    — 《真理与方法》

---

## 十二、论"实践智慧"（Phronesis）

25. "Phronesis is not theoretical knowledge but practical knowledge."
    — 《真理与方法》
    — 实践智慧不是理论知识，而是实践知识。

26. "Hermeneutics is the art of understanding what is said... and applying it to one's own situation."
    — 《真理与方法》

---

## 十三、论"对话与历史"

27. "A classical work is one that addresses itself... to the whole of history."
    — 《真理与方法》
    — 经典作品是那种对整个历史言说的作品。

28. "To be historical means to be aware of one's historicity."
    — 《真理与方法》/ p. 311

---

## 十四、论"美德"

29. "The hermeneutical virtues are: openness, listening, willingness to be convinced."
    — 多次访谈
    — 解释学的美德：开放、倾听、愿意被说服。

30. "Tolerance is not indifference but participation in a common search for truth."
    — 多次访谈
    — 容忍不是冷漠，而是参与共同寻求真理。
