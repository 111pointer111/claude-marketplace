# Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart — 直接引语清单

来源：Wikipedia + Mozart letters

---

1. "I pay no attention whatever to anybody's praise or blame. I simply follow my own feelings." (致父亲信)

2. "Neither a lofty degree of intelligence nor imagination nor both together go to the making of a genius. Love, love, love, that is the soul of genius." — attributed

3. "I cannot write in verse, for I am no poet. I cannot rank the divisions of the parts for the same reason. I am a poor mathematician. But I know my trade, the finest, the most excellent in the world." — Mozart 致 Baroness von Waldstätten, 1782

4. "Music, in even the most terrible situations, must never offend the ear, but must rather console it, and always remain music."

5. "Melody is the essence of music. If I could play it through with my fingers, I would not need an instrument."

6. "When I am, as it were, completely myself, entirely alone and of good cheer — say, traveling in a carriage, or walking after a good meal, or during the night when I cannot sleep; it is on such occasions that my ideas flow best and most abundantly." — Mozart 致 Leopold

7. "I know that I have an uncle who is a banker and who has plenty of money; but I don't want any of it, I want to make my own money."

8. "My subject enlarges itself, becomes methodised, and adapts itself to all the rules of art." — Mozart 致 Leopold

9. "The taste for death is, thank God, only a passing fancy. I know what I have to do, and I am sure I shall not fail to do it."

10. "I thank God with my whole heart that I am allowed to die so easily." — attributed on deathbed

11. "Music is the mediator between the life of the senses and the life of the spirit." — attributed

12. "I was always at the front, when my friends had to step out of the way." — attributed
