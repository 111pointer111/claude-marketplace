# Rudolf Carnap 直接引语

> 来源: SEP + Wikipedia
> 抓取日期: 2026-07-26

## 哲学观

- "philosophical inquiry became, for Carnap, a kind of 'conceptual engineering'" — SEP
- "there is no moral in logic" ——《语言的逻辑句法》(1934)

## 反形而上学

- "the elimination of metaphysics through logical analysis of language" — Carnap 著名论点
- (形而上学) "伪装成知识的失败艺术形式" (既是假艺术也是假知识)

## 《世界的逻辑构造》(Aufbau, 1928)

- "wherever possible, logical constructions are to be substituted for inferred entities"

## 《语言的逻辑句法》(1934)

- "we are not in the business of setting up prohibitions but of arriving at conventions…In logic there are no morals"
- (容忍原则) "language forms can be freely chosen"

## 内在与外在问题

- "It only made sense to speak of something 'existing' relative to a framework"

## 与 Quine / Tarski

- (Rose Rand 评) "Carnap's conception of semantics starts from the basis given in Tarski's work"
- (Quine 评价) "Two Dogmas of Empiricism" (1951) 批判分析-综合区分

## 维也纳学派宣言 (1929, 与 Hahn/Neurath 合著)

- "The scientific world-conception serves life, and life receives it."
