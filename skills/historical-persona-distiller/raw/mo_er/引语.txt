# G. E. Moore 直接引语

> 来源: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy + Wikipedia
> 抓取日期: 2026-07-26

## 反唯心论 (Refutation of Idealism)

- "when we try to introspect the sensation of blue, all we can see is the blue" (Refutation of Idealism, 41)
- "'blue' is as much an object… as the most exalted and independent real thing" (同上, 42)
- "it is indifferent to their nature whether anybody thinks them or not" (The Nature of Judgment, 4)

## 真理 (Truth, 1901-02)

- "a truth differs in no respect from the reality to which it was supposed merely to correspond" (Truth, 21)
- "That 'to be true' means to be thought in a certain way is, therefore, certainly false." (PE 183)

## 元伦理学 (Principia Ethica, 1903)

- (自然主义谬误 / 开放问题): "these properties, in fact, were simply not 'other,' but absolutely and entirely the same with goodness." (PE §10)
- (有机整体原则): "The value of a whole must not be assumed to be the same as the sum of the values of its parts." (*Principia*, §18)
- (后果主义): "Right actions are those producing the most good."

## 常识哲学 (A Defence of Common Sense, 1925)

- "the earth had existed also for many years before my body was born"
- "There exists at present a living human body, which is my body. This body was born at a certain time…"

## 外在世界 (Proof of an External World, 1939)

- "Here is one hand… and here is another"
- "How? By holding up my two hands" (Proof of an External World, 166)
- "How absurd it would be to suggest that I did not know it, but only believed it"

## Moore 悖论 (Moore's Paradox)

- "It is raining, but I do not believe it is raining"
