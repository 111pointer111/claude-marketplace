# G. E. Moore 传记

> 来源: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy + Wikipedia
> 抓取日期: 2026-07-26

## 基本信息

- 生于: 1873-11-04, Upper Norwood, 伦敦南部
- 卒于: 1958-10-24, 剑桥 (享年 84)
- 妻子: Dorothy Ely; 二子 (诗人 Nicholas Moore, 作曲家 Timothy Moore)
- 兄长: Thomas Sturge Moore (诗人/雕刻家)
- 教育: Dulwich College; 1892 入剑桥 Trinity College (古典学 + 道德科学)
- 一等双学位毕业 (1896)
- 剑桥 Fellow (1898); Mental Philosophy and Logic 教授 (1925-1939)
- 受业师: J.M.E. McTaggart (唯心论) 与 James Ward
- 剑桥使徒会成员 (1894-1901)
- *Mind* 主编; Aristotelian Society 主席 (1918-19); Ethical Union 主席 (1935-36)
- 1951 年由乔治六世授予 Order of Merit

## 思想发展轨迹

### 阶段一: 唯心论及其转向 (1892-1898)
- 1892 入剑桥, 受 McTaggart 影响接受 Bradley 式绝对唯心论
- 1897 Fellowship 论文仍持唯心论立场
- 1898 Fellowship 论文《伦理学之形上基础》开始怀疑, 转向反 Kant / 反 Bradley
- 核心冲击: 康德的 "现象 vs 本体"、Bradley 的 "内在关系"、McTaggart 的 "绝对"

### 阶段二: 反唯心论与《伦理学原理》 (1899-1910)
- 1899 《判断的性质》: 概念是客观非心理性, 思维 ≠ 客观对象
- 1903 《理想主义之反驳》: 论证 "esse est percipi" 蕴含矛盾
- 1903 *Principia Ethica*: 元伦理学奠基; 自然主义谬误 + 开放问题论证; 内在价值 vs 工具价值; 有机整体原则
- 1904 Fellowship 届满, 离开剑桥; 1905-1911 在外漂泊 (含 1910-11 哲学主要问题讲演)

### 阶段三: 哲学分析 (1911-1925)
- 1911 回剑桥任讲师
- 1912 *Ethics* (Home University Library)
- 与 Wittgenstein 交往 (1914 挪威笔记; Moore 建议 *Tractatus* 之名)
- 1918-39 任 Moral Sciences Club 主席
- 1921-47 任 *Mind* 主编
- 1922 *Philosophical Studies* 收录早期文章
- 1923-24 转向常识实在论 (Common Sense)

### 阶段四: 常识哲学与剑桥教授 (1925-1939)
- 1925 任 Mental Philosophy and Logic 教授 (继承自 James Ward)
- 1925 《为常识辩护》: 列举"地球在我出生前已存在多年"等老生常谈
- 1929 Wittgenstein 返剑桥, 同侪合作
- 1936 《存在是谓词吗?》反 Russell
- 1939 退休 (Wittgenstein 接任教授席位); 《外在世界之证明》: "Here is one hand"

### 阶段五: 晚期访美与身后出版 (1940-1959)
- 1940-44 访美讲学
- 1942 《我的批评者们》(*My Critics*)
- 1951 Order of Merit (乔治六世)
- 1953 *Some Main Problems of Philosophy* 身后出版
- 1958-10-24 逝世
- 1959 *Philosophical Papers* 身后出版 (含 *Proof of an External World*)

## 关键引语 (按主题)

### 反唯心论
- "when we try to introspect the sensation of blue, all we can see is the blue" (The Nature of Judgment / Refutation of Idealism 41)
- "'blue' is as much an object… as the most exalted and independent real thing" (同上, 42)
- "it is indifferent to their nature whether anybody thinks them or not" (The Nature of Judgment, 4)

### 真理 (Truth, 1901-02)
- "a truth differs in no respect from the reality to which it was supposed merely to correspond" (Truth, 21)
- "That 'to be true' means to be thought in a certain way is, therefore, certainly false." (PE 183)

### 元伦理学
- (开放问题): "these properties, in fact, were simply not 'other,' but absolutely and entirely the same with goodness." (PE §10)
- (有机整体): "The value of a whole must not be assumed to be the same as the sum of the values of its parts." (*Principia*, §18)
- (后果主义): "Right actions are those producing the most good." (PE 解读)

### 常识实在论
- "the earth had existed also for many years before my body was born" (Defence of Common Sense)
- "How? By holding up my two hands" (Proof of an External World 166)
- "How absurd it would be to suggest that I did not know it, but only believed it" (同上)

## 与同时代人物关系

- **Russell**: 早期引领 Moore 反唯心论; Moore 后批评 Russell 蕴含观与存在观但保持友谊; Russell 评价 Moore "He had a kind of exquisite purity" / "I almost worship him as if he were a god"
- **Wittgenstein**: 1914 挪威笔记; Moore 建议 *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus* 之名 (仿 Spinoza); Wittgenstein 接替 Moore 教授席位 (1939); Wittgenstein 在《论确定性》中回应 Moore; Wittgenstein 论 Moore 悖论: "the most impressive philosophical insight that Moore had ever introduced"
- **McTaggart**: 早期导师, 后反其唯心论
- **Bloomsbury Group**: 通过 Russell 加入, 与 Lytton Strachey、Leonard Woolf、Keynes 交往
- **W.D. Ross**: 批评 Moore 义务论忽视个人关系
- **A.J. Ayer, C.L. Stevenson**: 受 Moore 激发走向非认知主义
- **Ryle**: "He treated us as corrigible and therefore as responsible thinkers."

## 关键遗产

- **Moore's Paradox**: "It is raining, but I do not believe it is raining" — Wittgenstein 视为涉及自我认同的深层现象 (见《哲学研究》II.x)
- **元伦理学奠基**: 开放问题论证、自然主义谬误、supervenience 关系
- **分析哲学方法论典范**: 与 Russell / Wittgenstein / Frege 并列
- **常识实在论传统**: 拒斥唯心论与怀疑论

## 关键引语 (Russell / Wittgenstein / 后人评价)

- Russell: "I almost worship him as if he were a god. I have never felt such an extravagant admiration for anybody." / "He had a kind of exquisite purity."
- Wittgenstein: "the most impressive philosophical insight that Moore had ever introduced"
- Ryle: "He treated us as corrigible and therefore as responsible thinkers."
- Ray Monk: "the most revered philosopher of his era"
- Keynes: Moore 之 *Principia Ethica* 价值清单是世俗"宗教"
- Moore 接受 Order of Merit 后对妻言: "Do you know that the King had never heard of Wittgenstein!"
