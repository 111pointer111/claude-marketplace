# 一、传记

## 基本信息

- 生于: 1873-11-04, Upper Norwood, 伦敦南部
- 卒于: 1958-10-24, 剑桥 (享年 84)
- 妻子: Dorothy Ely; 二子 (诗人 Nicholas Moore, 作曲家 Timothy Moore)
- 兄长: Thomas Sturge Moore (诗人/雕刻家)
- 教育: Dulwich College; 1892 入剑桥 Trinity College (古典学 + 道德科学)
- 一等双学位毕业 (1896)
- 剑桥 Fellow (1898); Mental Philosophy and Logic 教授 (1925-1939)
- 受业师: J.M.E. McTaggart (唯心论) 与 James Ward
- 剑桥使徒会成员 (1894-1901)
- *Mind* 主编; Aristotelian Society 主席 (1918-19); Ethical Union 主席 (1935-36)
- 1951 年由乔治六世授予 Order of Merit

## 年表

1873 生; 1892 入剑桥; 1894-1901 使徒会; 1896 一等毕业; 1897 首竞 Fellowship 持唯心论; 1898 竞 Fellowship 成功转向反 Kant/Bradley; 1899 《判断的性质》; 1901-02 《真理》; 1903 《理想主义之反驳》+ *Principia Ethica*; 1904 离开剑桥; 1905-06 知觉对象; 1908 评 James 实用主义; 1910-11 主要问题讲演; 1911 回剑桥任讲师; 1912 *Ethics*; 1912-44 Moral Sciences Club 主席; 1914 访 Wittgenstein 于挪威; 1918 《某些知觉判断》; 1918-19 Aristotelian Society 主席; 1919-20 《外在与内在关系》; 1921 任 *Mind* 主编; 1922 *Philosophical Studies*; 1923 普遍 vs 特殊; 1925 任教授+《为常识辩护》; 1929 Wittgenstein 返剑桥; 1935-36 Ethical Union 主席; 1936 《存在是谓词吗》; 1939 退休+《外在世界之证明》; 1940-44 访美; 1942 《我的批评者们》; 1951 Order of Merit; 1953 *Some Main Problems of Philosophy*; 1958-10-24 逝世; 1959 *Philosophical Papers*.

## 主要著作

- *The Nature of Judgment* (1899)
- *Principia Ethica* (1903)
- *The Refutation of Idealism* (1903)
- *Kant's Idealism* (1904)
- *The Nature and Reality of the Objects of Perception* (1905–06)
- *Professor James' 'Pragmatism'* (1908)
- *Ethics* (1912)
- *Some Judgments of Perception* (1918)
- *Philosophical Studies* (1922)
- *A Defence of Common Sense* (1925)
- *Are the Characteristics of Things Universal or Particular?* (1923)
- *Some Main Problems of Philosophy* (1953, 1910-11 讲演)
- *Proof of an External World* (in *Philosophical Papers*, 1959)

# 二、直接引语

## 反唯心论
- "when we try to introspect the sensation of blue, all we can see is the blue"
- "'blue' is as much an object… as the most exalted and independent real thing"
- "it is indifferent to their nature whether anybody thinks them or not"

## 真理
- "a truth differs in no respect from the reality to which it was supposed merely to correspond"
- "That 'to be true' means to be thought in a certain way is, therefore, certainly false."

## 元伦理学
- "these properties, in fact, were simply not 'other,' but absolutely and entirely the same with goodness." (PE §10)
- "The value of a whole must not be assumed to be the same as the sum of the values of its parts." (*Principia* §18)
- "Right actions are those producing the most good."

## 常识哲学
- "the earth had existed also for many years before my body was born"
- "There exists at present a living human body, which is my body."

## 外在世界
- "Here is one hand… and here is another"
- "How? By holding up my two hands"
- "How absurd it would be to suggest that I did not know it, but only believed it"

## Moore 悖论
- "It is raining, but I do not believe it is raining"

## 同时代人评价
- Russell: "I almost worship him as if he were a god"
- Russell: "He had a kind of exquisite purity"
- Wittgenstein: "the most impressive philosophical insight that Moore had ever introduced"
- Ryle: "He treated us as corrigible and therefore as responsible thinkers"
- Moore: "Do you know that the King had never heard of Wittgenstein!"

# 三、后世评述

- 与 Russell、Wittgenstein 组成 Trinity College 三巨头
- Ray Monk 称之为 "the most revered philosopher of his era"
- 与 Russell、Wittgenstein、Frege 并列为分析哲学开创者
- 主要批评者: W.D. Ross, A.J. Ayer, Wittgenstein (晚年分歧)
- 终结英国唯心论在剑桥的统治地位
- 1951 Order of Merit — 主流学界对哲学家的最高英国荣誉
- 后世影响: 元伦理学、Moore's Paradox、常识实在论、伦理直觉主义

## 思想史意义

终结英国唯心论 + 开启分析哲学 + 通过 Bloomsbury Group 影响 Keynes 等政策实践
