# 一、生平传记

## 童年与母亲 (1821-1839)

Born 9 April 1821 in Paris. Father Joseph-François died February 1827. Mother Caroline married Lieutenant Colonel Jacques Aupick 1828 — formative trauma. Educated at Lyon, then Lycée Louis-le-Grand in Paris. Rejected law career 1839.

## 印度之旅与放荡 (1841-1847)

Stepfather sent him to India 1841; disembarked in Mauritius. Returned 1842, inherited fortune, squandered it in dandy life. Met Jeanne Duval c. 1842. Family placed property in trust 1844. Used laudanum/hashish 1843-1845. Salon art criticism established reputation 1845-46. La Fanfarlo (1847).

## 革命与艺术 (1848-1857)

Briefly participated in Revolutions of 1848 — confirmed by Britannica. Took part in workers' uprising June 1848 and resistance to Bonapartist coup December 1851. Thereafter claimed to lose interest in politics, focused on writing. Translated Poe's works 1847-1865. Published Les Fleurs du mal 1857 — six poems banned, fined, prosecution.

## 后期与衰亡 (1857-1867)

His stepfather died 1857; reconciled with mother Caroline, lived at Honfleur. Published revised edition 1861. Refused Legion of Honor. Departed for Belgium 1864. Suffered massive stroke 1866 — paralysis and aphasia. Died Paris 31 August 1867, age 46. Mother Caroline died 16 August 1871.

# 二、核心贡献

## 诗集

- Les Fleurs du mal (1857/1861) — 19 世纪最重要诗集之一
- Les Épaves (1866) — 包含被禁六首
- Le Spleen de Paris / Petits Poèmes en prose (1868 出版) — 散文诗

## 散文

- La Fanfarlo (1847) — 中篇小说
- Le Peintre de la vie moderne (1863) — 现代性理论
- Les Paradis artificiels (1860) — 关于药物体验
- Mon cœur mis à nu / Fusées (死后出版) — 私人日志

## 翻译

- E.A. 坡短篇小说集（1856-1865）
- 托马斯·德·昆西
- 维克多·雨果（部分）

## 艺术评论

- Salon of 1845
- Salon of 1846
- Salon of 1859 — 含为马奈《奥林匹亚》的著名辩护
