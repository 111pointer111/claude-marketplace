# 一、传记

## 基本信息
- **Michael Sandel** (1953-03-05 出生)
- 美国政治哲学家, 社群主义代表

## 教育
- Brandeis University, B.A. in politics, 1975 (Phi Beta Kappa)
- Rhodes Scholar, Balliol College, Oxford; DPhil under Charles Taylor, 1981

## 学术生涯
- Harvard 1981 起
- Anne T. and Robert M. Bass Professor of Government
- *Justice* 课程 15,000+ 学生, 全球数千万观众

## 核心思想
1. 社群主义 (但不太舒服于此标签)
2. 对 Rawls 的批判
3. 正义 vs 共同善
4. 市场与道德
5. 反完美主义
6. 公共哲学
7. 精英统治批判

## 主要著作
- *Liberalism and the Limits of Justice* (1982)
- *Democracy's Discontent* (1996, new edition 2022)
- *Public Philosophy* (2005)
- *The Case Against Perfection* (2007)
- *Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do?* (2009)
- *What Money Can't Buy* (2012)
- *The Tyranny of Merit* (2020)

## 公共服务
- 2001-2009 Bush 总统生物伦理委员会成员

## 荣誉
- 1985 Harvard-Radcliffe Phi Beta Kappa Teaching Prize
- 2012 *Foreign Policy* Top Global Thinker
- 2018 Premio Princesa de Asturias
- 2025 Berggruen Prize

# 二、直接引语

- discontent takes "the form of inchoate anxieties—a growing sense that we were losing control of the forces that govern our lives" ——《Democracy's Discontent》(1996)
- "elites have been insulated from the accountability that a broader conception of the common good requires" ——《The Tyranny of Merit》(2020)
- Rawls 的"无知之幕"把人们视为 "unencumbered selves"

- 当代最有影响力的政治哲学家之一
- 社群主义代表
- 公共哲学的倡导者
- 1982 对 Rawls 的经典批判
