# Michel Foucault - Wikipedia Biography

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Foucault

## Biography Overview

Paul-Michel Foucault (15 October 1926 – 25 June 1984) was a French historian of ideas and philosopher, also an author, literary critic, political activist, and teacher. His theories primarily addressed relationships between power, knowledge, and liberty, analyzing how they are used as social control through multiple institutions.

## Life Chronology

**Early life (1926–1951)**
- Born in Poitiers, France into upper-middle-class family
- Educated at Lycée Henri-IV, École Normale Supérieure, University of Paris
- Came under influence of Jean Hyppolite and Louis Althusser
- Joined French Communist Party in 1950; left in 1953
- Obtained DES in Philosophy 1949; agrégation in 1951

**Early career (1951–1960)**
- Worked as psychology instructor at ENS (1951–55)
- Taught at Université de Lille (1953–54)
- Cultural diplomat at University of Uppsala, Sweden
- Worked at University of Warsaw, Poland (1958)
- Director of Institut français Hamburg (1958–1960)

**Growing career (1960–1970)**
- Published *Madness and Civilization* (1961)
- Taught at University of Clermont-Ferrand (1960–1966)
- Published *The Birth of the Clinic* (1963) and *The Order of Things* (1966)
- Lectured at University of Tunis (1966–1968)
- Head of philosophy at Paris VIII/Vincennes

**Later life (1970–1984)**
- Elected to Collège de France (1970)
- Published *Discipline and Punish* (1975)
- Published *The History of Sexuality* Vol. 1 (1976)
- Reported on Iranian Revolution (1978)
- Died in Paris from HIV/AIDS complications

## Major Works

- *Madness and Civilization* (1961)
- *The Birth of the Clinic* (1963)
- *The Order of Things* (1966)
- *Discipline and Punish* (1975)
- *The History of Sexuality* (1976)

## Notable Ideas

Biopower, disciplinary institution, discourse analysis, discursive formation, dispositif, épistémè, archaeology, genealogy, carceral archipelago, governmentality, heterotopia, gaze, power-knowledge, panopticism, subjectivation, parrhesia, epimeleia heautou, regimes of truth, Foucault's boomerang.

> "Marxism exists in nineteenth-century thought as a fish exists in water; that is, it ceases to breathe anywhere else."

> "There exists an international citizenry that has its rights, and has its duties, and that is committed to rise up against every abuse of power."

## Schools and Influences

Continental philosophy, French Nietzscheanism, post-structuralism. Influenced by Hegel, Marx, Kant, Husserl, Heidegger, Nietzsche, Freud, Binswanger, Canguilhem, Althusser, Hyppolite.

## Death and Legacy

Foucault died in Paris from complications of HIV/AIDS. He became the first public figure in France to die from complications of the disease. His partner Daniel Defert founded the AIDES charity in his memory. His work influenced anthropology, communication studies, criminology, cultural studies, feminism, literary theory, psychology, and sociology.
