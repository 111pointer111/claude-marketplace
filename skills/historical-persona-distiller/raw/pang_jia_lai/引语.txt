# 庞加莱 直接引语与名言

## 一、庞加莱本人语录

> Mathematicians do not study objects, but relations between objects.

> Science is built up of facts, as a house is built of stones; but an accumulation of facts is no more a science than a heap of stones is a house.

> The scientist does not study nature because it is useful to do so. He studies it because he takes pleasure in it, and he takes pleasure in it because it is beautiful.

> It is through science that we have glimpses of a great poet who has written in mathematical language.

> Mathematical discoveries, small or great, are never born of pure reason alone — they always come from imagination first.

> Le mathématicien pur doit oublier qu'il y a des choses contingentes; il doit voir les choses du point de vue de l'éternel.

> L'analyse n'est qu'une extension de l'arithmétique.

> L'avenir de la mathématique, c'est l'avenir de la pensée scientifique tout entière.

> Les mathématiques sont l'art de donner le même nom à des choses différentes.

> L'intuition ne peut s'exercer qu'avec le secours de la sensibilité.

> La géométrie est l'art de bien conduire son raison dans les choses de l'espace.

> If we knew the position and velocity of every atom in the universe, we could predict the future completely — but our predictions would be limited by our own ignorance of these conditions.

> "The Last Universalist" — Common epithet for Poincaré

> "the Gauss of modern mathematics" — Common epithet for Poincaré

> "monster of mathematics" — Poincaré's math teacher

> "the greatest philosopher of science of all time" — Karl Popper about Poincaré
