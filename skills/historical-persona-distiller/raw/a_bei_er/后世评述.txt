# Niels Henrik Abel — 后世评述

> 来自 Wikipedia "Niels Henrik Abel" 词条中的他人评价段。

---

## Adrien-Marie Legendre (French mathematician)

- Called Abel's work on elliptic functions **"a monument more lasting than bronze"**
  — quoted in Wikipedia biography, after Abel's death

## Charles Hermite (French mathematician)

- **"Abel has left mathematicians enough to keep them busy for five hundred years."**
  — Wikipedia biography

## August Leopold Crelle (German mathematician)

- Founder of Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (1826), where Abel contributed 7 articles in the first volume
- Personally offered Abel a professorship at the University of Berlin — the letter arrived two days after Abel's death on 6 April 1829

## Augustin-Louis Cauchy (French mathematician)

- Set aside Abel's memoir on the addition of algebraic differentials submitted to the French Academy of Sciences
- It was forgotten until after Abel's death
- The episode is often cited as a tragic example of institutional neglect of genius

## Felix Klein (German mathematician, 1849-1925)

- Compared Abel's productivity and personality to Mozart — both early-genius who died young
- Noted that the Abel monument in Oslo is disappointing compared to the genius

## 现代荣誉

- Abel Prize in mathematics — first awarded in 2003
- Many mathematical concepts bear his name: Abelian groups (lowercase 'a' by convention), Abel-Ruffini theorem, Abel's theorem, Abel's summation formula, Abel's test, Abelian functions
- Wikipedia: "His legacy endures in the Abel Prize in mathematics, first awarded in 2003, and in the many mathematical concepts that bear his name."

## 评价综览

- Abel 是 19 世纪最伟大的数学家之一，但因 26 岁早逝未能发挥全部潜力
- Cauchy's neglect 是数学史最著名的悲剧之一
- Abel-Ruffini 定理终结了 250 年的五次方程求根式解探索
- Abelian groups、elliptic functions 是现代代数几何、密码学的基石
